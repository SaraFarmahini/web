AutoRelate — personal concept project

AutoRelate is an imaginary automotive marketplace Mahyar developed as a personal project alongside his interest in European vehicle platforms. The mockup explores how a listings experience could feel calmer, clearer, and more trust-oriented — without the noise of a typical classifieds interface.

Disclaimer: AutoRelate is a fictional brand and site name only. It is not a real company or product, is not affiliated with any existing marketplace, and exists for visual communication and strategic discussion. It is not a production-ready implementation and is not intended to present Mahyar as a UI designer or frontend developer.

Focus areas

  • Reducing cognitive overload
  • Improving listing scanability
  • Simplifying browsing flow
  • Strengthening trust perception
  • Clearer commercial conversion paths

Automotive Interest

Mahyar has a long-standing personal interest in cars that sits apart from marketplace operations and commercial delivery. He enjoys learning about automotive history, classical cars, vehicle engineering, and the technical fundamentals of how automobiles work — as well as the evolution of brands and design over time.

That curiosity runs from the mechanics of real vehicles to the way listings and customer journeys behave online: browsing and analysing car listings is as natural as reading up on technical and mechanical issues, engineering choices, and market context. He often spends time in automotive museums and exhibitions, where heritage and mechanical identity come into focus away from day-to-day commercial pressure.

The thread is continuity between professional marketplace experience and a genuine, patient interest in the industry — not spectacle, but context and depth.
